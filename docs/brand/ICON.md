# Icono de 800NK ADV Link

El arte maestro está en:

`app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/ic_launcher_foreground_art.png`

Android lo usa como capa frontal adaptativa sobre un fondo carbón definido en
`app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_launcher_background.xml`. El margen de seguridad se aplica en
`app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_launcher_foreground.xml` para evitar recortes en launchers circulares.

La imagen fue generada con GPT y no utiliza el logotipo de CFMOTO ni pretende representar una
aplicación oficial.

## Prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: Android adaptive launcher icon foreground, production-ready app icon source
Primary request: Create a bold, original emblem for an app named "800NK ADV Link", designed
specifically for a modern naked motorcycle connectivity and Android Auto app.
Subject: A symmetrical, highly simplified front view of an angular streetfighter motorcycle.
Combine two sharp headlight shapes with a slim vertical dashboard shape in the center and two
subtle wireless-link arcs.
Style/medium: Flat vector-like logo mark, crisp geometric edges, minimal detail, strong silhouette,
premium motorsport technology aesthetic.
Composition/framing: Perfectly centered square composition. Keep the complete mark inside the
central 60% of the canvas with genuine transparent padding. It must remain recognizable at 48 px.
Color palette: Bright cyan/teal, safety orange, white/light gray, and charcoal. Maximum four solid
colors.
Background: genuinely transparent RGBA.
Text: none.
Constraints: original artwork; clean closed shapes; no thin lines; no gradients; no shadows; no
glow; no 3D; no enclosing circle, rounded-square tile, or background badge.
Avoid: CFMOTO logo, manufacturer logos, copied motorcycle badges, words, letters, numbers,
watermarks, photorealism, mirrors, handlebars, suspension details, and tire tread.
```
